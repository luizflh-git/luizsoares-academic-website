factual claim on the site comes from, per three categories:

the paper was a **conference paper** at the 2014 IEEE Symposium on
Differential Evolution (part of IEEE SSCI 2014), not a journal article in
T-CIAIG. The site publishes the verified version (Peer-Reviewed
Publications on `publications.qmd`); the CV PDF itself was left as provided
per policy (files aren't edited), but Luiz should regenerate/correct it.
